Release v2.2.0 preserves all 20,164 SPC tornado rows from 2010–2025, including unknown EF ratings. NCEI annual all-hazard archives and exact tornado extracts, generation-pinned NOAA Event Footprint Catalog files, Census county estimates and the generalized 2020 county map remain the backbone. Conservative time/geometry matching retains accepted, ambiguous and unmatched links; EF ratings never determine matching. County totals are context, not people or buildings struck.

NOAA SWDI NEXRAD Level III TVS, mesocyclone and storm-structure records are associated within 20 km and 60 minutes before/after reported onset. IEM supplies NWS tornado/severe-thunderstorm warning updates. USGS Annual NLCD prior-year land cover and imperviousness are summarized at native 30 m resolution over accepted damage footprints or a 500 m fallback track buffer. Collection completed for radar/warnings for all events and NLCD for 20,147; 17 are outside selected coverage. A completed query does not guarantee a detection or nonmissing feature.

Seventeen linked analysis tables retain source IDs, geometry, provenance and coverage. Two reproducible event-level ML views preserve every event and final EF target. The onset predictor allowlist uses only the pre-cutoff subset, assumes five-minute radar latency, and anchors on final-catalog onset/location. Warning cancellation coverage is incomplete in this archive path. Path-dependent land cover, final dimensions and other post-event fields belong only in retrospective analyses. EF is a damage assessment, not a direct peak-wind measurement. ERA5 and ACS/TIGER tract enrichment are deferred.

The release bundles required warning text/schema assets, excludes working caches, and preserves checksums and extraction settings. Both published downloads were checked against one shared manifest; the hosted notebook verifies selected files and target preservation. Group related events for evaluation and review missingness and leakage before modeling. Code, detailed methods and immutable release receipts: https://github.com/jakeryderv/us-tornado-data-2010-2025
